The Shimansky Story Since 1991

The Beginning. The Shimansky story began in 1991, when founder and CEO Yair Shimansky arrived in South Africa after spending two years in Japan. The time spent in Japan served not only as a rite of passage into the world of diamonds and jewellery, but also as a major learning curve. It was here that he studied the science of light & optics in diamonds and where the Japanese culture of perfection made a life-long impression on him. Having learnt the intricacies of diamond qualities and the importance of cut and proportion, he was armed with a unique perspective on diamonds.


Upon his arrival in South Africa, Shimansky started designing and crafting jewellery which he sold at a craft market for one year before opening a small outlet in a popular shopping mall. This lead to the opening of the first Shimansky boutique store in 1994. In 1998 Shimansky decided to travel to the diamond mines in Kimberley, South Africa, and buy rough diamonds directly from the mines. The first batch of diamonds was given to a local polisher who polished the diamonds for weight gain and not to maximize their brilliance. This is when Shimansky decided to enter the world of diamond cutting and polishing and setup the first Shimansky diamond polishing workshop in 1999, unique among jewellers to this day. Shimansky now is a leading premium jewellery brand .

Responsibility That Matters

Every Shimansky diamond is traded, sourced and certified with the same care we put into cutting it. In short, here's what that means:

  • Kimberley Process compliant — every diamond is traded fairly and transparently, and guaranteed conflict-free.
  • Sourced directly from the mine — Shimansky is one of the rare few jewellers licensed to buy rough diamonds straight from well-known South African diamond fields.
  • Ethical Compliance certificate — every diamond is accompanied by our written guarantee of conflict-free origin, in accordance with the Kimberley Process.
  • GIA certified — independently graded by the Gemological Institute of America for cut, colour, clarity and carat weight.
  • Community impact — supporting the people and economies behind every stone, from mine to finger, cut and polished in-house in Cape Town.
