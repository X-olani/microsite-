Our Story

A Singular Journey
from Mine to Finger

Founded in South Africa, a land enriched by diamonds, gold, and platinum — Shimansky is renowned as the foremost premium jewellery brand, celebrated for iconic design and exceptional craftsmanship. Our singular involvement in every stage of a diamond’s journey from mine to finger ensures that only the most exceptional stones are selected and polished in-house for our jewellery collections.

Over 25 remarkable years, Shimansky has pioneered the use of platinum in fine jewellery, while also developing internationally patented diamond cuts and jewellery designs, setting the brand apart on the global stage. We have had the privilege of hosting guests from every corner of the world, offering a truly distinctive South African luxury experience and honouring significant moments with a unique Shimansky creation.

We look forward to delivering an exceptional, memorable experience for your guests.

The Cape Town Diamond Museum invites guests into a world of rare beauty, heritage, and craftsmanship. Set in the elegant Clock Tower at the iconic V&A Waterfront Precinct, with Table Mountain as its backdrop; this experience is intimate, exclusive, and an elegant highlight for discerning visitors to Cape Town.

Experience

An interactive museum visit: a refined journey into South African diamonds, from ancient beginnings to exceptional Shimansky creations.

Explore

Guests explore 3.3 billion years of diamond history and uncover the legacy of the Kimberley Diamond Rush.

Discover

The mine-to-finger journey firsthand, watching rough diamonds transformed into handcrafted masterpieces.

Learn

What is a diamond, where is it from, and how does it form?

Diamond Cutting & Polishing Factory

The art of polishing and shaping diamonds is a precise and exact science, requiring years of experience to master. Witness first-hand how skilled diamond cutters transform rough diamonds into exquisite creations of fire.

Guests also watch master craftsmen hand-make jewellery creations and see methods used to achieve Shimansky’s signature designs.

Manufacturing Workshop

Watch as master craftsmen hand-make stunning jewellery creations and see some of the methods used to achieve Shimansky’s signature designs.
