Our Story

A Singular Journey
from Mine to Finger

Founded in South Africa, a land enriched by diamonds, gold, and platinum — Shimansky is renowned as the foremost premium jewellery brand, celebrated for iconic design and exceptional craftsmanship. Our singular involvement in every stage of a diamond’s journey from mine to finger ensures that only the most exceptional stones are selected and polished in-house for our jewellery collections.

Over 25 remarkable years, Shimansky has pioneered the use of platinum in fine jewellery, while also developing internationally patented diamond cuts and jewellery designs, setting the brand apart on the global stage. We have had the privilege of hosting guests from every corner of the world, offering a truly distinctive South African luxury experience and honouring significant moments with a unique Shimansky creation.

We look forward to delivering an exceptional, memorable experience for your guests.

The Cape Town Diamond Museum invites guests into a world of rare beauty, storied heritage, and exceptional craftsmanship. Nestled within the elegant Clock Tower at the iconic V&A Waterfront, with the dramatic silhouette of Table Mountain as its backdrop, this is an intimate, exclusive, and quietly spectacular highlight for the discerning visitor.

Far more than a museum visit, this is a refined odyssey into the story of South African diamonds — from their ancient geological origins to their transformation into extraordinary Shimansky creations. It is one seamless destination: museum, factory, and showroom, united under one distinguished roof.

The Tanzanite Standard

Tanzanite is a unique and alluring gem with a vibrant shade of colour ranging from rich royal blues to delicate shades of violet and hues of red. As a discerning gemstone buyer, you want to make sure that you are getting the best quality tanzanite available on the market. Important factors to consider when buying tanzanite include ethical sourcing, quality of the cut, and the importance of colour.

Orders processed in South Africa will take 5–7 business days to arrive. Overseas deliveries can take anywhere from 7–14 days. We guarantee a 14-day delivery on most orders.

Sustainability & Ethical Sourcing

At the heart of every Shimansky jewellery creation — across diamonds, gemstones, and corporate governance — we strive to act in a sustainable way to reduce any negative impact on the world, from our choice of light source, recycling of waste, and packaging material, to low and efficient energy processes and the careful selection of our service providers.

Our diamonds originate mainly from alluvial diamond mines where the old riverbed is mined with a strict code of rehabilitation after every digging takes place, filling back the land. The same applies to responsible gold and platinum mining.
