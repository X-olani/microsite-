Our Brand

Our Story

Founded in South Africa, a country rich in diamonds, gold and platinum, Shimansky is a leading premium jewellery brand sought after for iconic designs and expert craftsmanship. Our involvement in every step of a diamond's journey, from mine to finger, ensures that only the finest diamonds are selected and polished in-house.


Over the past 25 years, we have pioneered the use of platinum in South African jewellery and created copyright protected designs, marking our customers' special moments with a unique Shimansky creation.

The Shimansky Foundation

Giving Back

The Shimansky Diamond Foundation is a non-profit organisation supporting local causes, mainly focused on providing food and education to women and children in need. We channel any donation without administrative fees — 100% of the funds are used for the purpose they are intended for.


Purchase our My Africa, My Shimansky bracelets to support our initiatives — all proceeds go directly to the Foundation.
